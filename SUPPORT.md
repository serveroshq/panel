# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Check the [docs](https://docs.serveros.com) first, then ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/discussions) or on [Discord](https://discord.gg/serveros).
- **Something's broken:** [open a bug report](https://github.com/serveroshq/panel/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with the steps to reproduce it.
- **Security problem:** don't post it publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Your serveros.com account, billing or ServerOS Cloud:** email [support@serveros.com](mailto:support@serveros.com). Issues here are for the open source panel's code.

Help in Discussions and on Discord comes from the community, and from the team when we can.
